Fuzzy search for every input: what should a query find, and in what order?

Tinycast matches typed text three different ways today. The launcher uses a strong word-start aligner, menus, notes and settings use a simpler tiered matcher, and the clipboard, snippets and quicklinks only find text that appears exactly as typed. Pick a search box, type a query, and compare four matchers side by side, including a proposed single matcher for every input. The question: does an fzf-style matcher (with an exact-words profile for the clipboard) find what you expect, without flooding the results or burying recent items?
